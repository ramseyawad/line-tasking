How to use Line Tasking

Press ? any time

1 · Start of shift

  • Check the bar under the header shows the right crew letter, shift and date (e.g. A Shift · Lates · Tue 24 Nov). If not, press Correct.
  • Check who's in: Shift list tab → pick the day and shift and press Apply, or flick people on/off with the switches.
  • Pick your view next to Jobs: Line tasks (line controller), Certifier (maintenance controller) or All. Each screen remembers its own choice.
  • Unfinished jobs from the last shift are already on the board.

2 · Raise a job

  • Raise a job (or press N). Pick the tail, bay, priority, state and area.
  • Choose Line task or Certifier – it applies to every line you type.
  • Type one task per line. Start a line with INFO for a note rather than a task (e.g. INFO crew in at 1400).
  • Status notes (WATER DUMPED, NO APU…) go in the notes box, separated by commas.
  • Pick engineers by trade at the bottom if you already know who – optional.
  • Ctrl + Enter adds it.

3 · Crew a job

  • Each card has B1, B2, Mechs (and Other) boxes at the top – use + Add in the right box. It only offers people on shift in that trade.
  • Assigning doesn't make them busy. They stay available until a line they're on is put in progress – so you can line people up for the next task.
  • Work starts per task: tap a line's circle to put it in progress – that starts the clock for the people on that line. Anyone already on another started job shows on ZM… next to their name. When someone has finished all the lines assigned to them, their name turns green – ✓ done – free – and they show as available again.
  • Press × on a name to take them off.
  • To put someone on just one line, use + assign at the right of that line.

4 · Task lines

  • Click a line's text to edit just that line. Enter saves, Esc cancels. Clear the text and press Enter to remove the line.
  • + Add a task at the bottom of a card adds a line (Enter to add, the box stays open for the next one).
  • The Line / Cert dropdown on each line marks who it belongs to. Cert lines show amber.

5 · Progress

  • Tap the circle on a line to move it on: ○ Not started → ▶ In progress → ✓ Done.
  • Each aircraft is one tile. Raising a job on a tail that already has a tile adds the tasks to it. When every line is done the tile shows All tasks done and stays until handover. CRS marks the aircraft Tech ready (green).
  • Move lines on honestly – the times feed the diary and the Stats tab.

6 · Card details

  • P = priority (1 is highest – sorts to the top).
  • State: Serviceable / AOG / Shed Maint – AOG time is recorded for Stats. Owner: who has the aircraft (Line, AAG, Retrofit, Deployed, Casualty Bay…) – per tail, same on every card.
  • Sentence… (under the state) is the Airbus sentencing code for the aircraft – it belongs to the tail, so it's the same on every card for that aircraft. Change it whenever the aircraft's situation changes; the hours in each code show in Stats → Aircraft sentencing, where you can also set tails that have no job open.
  • Status notes: type a note and press Enter; × removes it.
  • Change the tail or bay straight on the card.

7 · Finding things

  • The aircraft buttons (400–421, Off A/C) float at the top on every tab – tap one to jump to its card. Colours: orange in work, purple not started, amber unmanned, red ring AOG.
  • Notes at the end of that row jumps to the shift notes.
  • Filters next to Jobs: Open / In work / Not started / Unmanned / Done this shift. Search box finds tail, bay, task or name.
  • Switch between squares and stretched layouts with the two icons.
  • Edit tile layout (the four-box icon next to them – Line Control, SLE or admin): drag the boxes on a sample tile to move them, drag a box's right edge to resize, Hide what you don't need, then Save for all tiles. Reset to default puts it back.

Aircraft owners

  • Every aircraft has an owner – Line (us), Line Support, AAG, Retrofit, Deployed, Casualty Bay (admins can edit the list). Change it on the card or by tapping the aircraft in the top row.
  • The dark dropdown at the start of the aircraft row switches between owners (or All aircraft). The board, shift bar, shift notes, Export and Hand over follow that owner – each owner has its own shift, crew letter, notes and diary.
  • Tap any aircraft – even with no work – for its panel: owner, sentence, open jobs and Raise a job on this tail.
  • Each user has a section (set by an admin in Users – e.g. Kev: Line, SLE). You can change your own section's aircraft; other sections' aircraft are greyed in the top row and view only on the board. Admins can edit everything.
  • The wall board only shows Line aircraft. 6S reset is Line only.

8 · New & updated highlights

  • Everything is live – changes appear on everyone's screen within a second (green dot = connected).
  • A job someone else adds glows blue – NEW. One they change shows purple – UPDATED with what changed and who did it.
  • New lines get a blue NEW ✕ badge – click it to clear that line. ✓ Seen clears the card, mark all seen clears everything.
  • A blue dot on an aircraft button means something on it you haven't seen.

9 · Team & manning

  • Team button (or T) slides out who's free, who's working and who's on line roles.
  • The manning boxes show each trade against target/minimum – Set targets to change them. All A certs count together.
  • Line walkers (LWL) are on shift but not offered for tasks.
  • 22:00–02:00: lates and nights overlap – the other shift's rostered people are available too, marked N or L.

10 · Send to group

  • Send to group (top) sends every manned tail with its engineers, e.g. ZM420: Kyle J, Paul E. The paper-plane on a card sends just that one.
  • On a phone it opens your share sheet; on a laptop it copies – paste into the group chat.

11 · Wall board

  • Wall board (top right) goes full screen for the hangar screen. It shows manned jobs only: red = not started, orange = in progress, green = complete.
  • Move the mouse to see the controls: scroll speed, squares/rows, exit (Esc).
  • It updates live and picks up new app versions by itself.

12 · Shift notes & handover

  • Shift notes (bottom of the board) carry through every shift and save to each diary. Useful info tab is for standing info (contacts, reminders).
  • Export so far previews the diary at any time.
  • 6S reset (shift bar, top) – open each area's form (Line Controller, Flight Line, Office, Crew Room & Kitchen, In Use POL), complete it, then tick it off. Ticks are shared, show on the handover and in the diary, and reset for the next shift.
  • Hand over shift: enter the off-going and on-coming names and sign (or tick not available with a reason), pick the crew coming on, then hand over. The diary is saved permanently and opens to print or save as PDF.
  • Unfinished jobs carry over to the next shift automatically.

13 · Other tabs

  • Shift list – the roster by trade, who's on shift, import a new roster.
  • History – every completed job, who did it and how long; export CSV.
  • Stats – hours per engineer and aircraft, turnaround, busiest hours, carry-overs, shifts below minimum; pick a date range; export CSV.
  • Diary – every handed-over shift, read-only.
  • Useful info → Move request – fill in the tow request; Submit opens your email to Eng Ops (DEOCs) and Serco Ramp with the subject set, and copies the formatted form – click in the message and paste (Ctrl+V / ⌘V). Download Outlook draft gives a fully filled email that opens in Outlook desktop.
  • Users (admin) – approve profile requests and manage access.
